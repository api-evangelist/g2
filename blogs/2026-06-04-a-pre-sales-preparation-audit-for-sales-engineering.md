This article presents a pre-sales audit framework for sales engineers, illustrated by a case study where a Sales Engineer spent 12 hours preparing for a single 90-minute demo. The author outlines a structured checklist approach to reduce prep time while improving demo quality and win rates. The guide is aimed at sales engineering teams looking to standardize their discovery, qualification, and technical preparation workflows.
