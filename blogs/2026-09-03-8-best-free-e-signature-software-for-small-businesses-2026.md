Not all "free" e-signature tools are free in the same way, and for a small business, that distinction is the whole game. After testing, I found Box Sign, DocuSign, Dropbox Sign, Foxit eSign, Jotform Sign, PandaDoc, SignNow, and Xodo Sign to be the best free e-signature software for small businesses in 2026. Each one turned out to have a distinct sweet spot worth knowing before you commit.
