TL;DR: How do I automate financial reporting? To automate financial reporting, centralize and connect your data sources, implement an FP&A or financial reporting platform , configure automated workflows and report templates, and schedule secure report delivery. Done right, this eliminates manual data pulls, reduces reporting cycle time from days to hours, and frees the finance team to focus on analysis rather than assembly.
