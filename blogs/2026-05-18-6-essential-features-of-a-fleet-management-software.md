The essential fleet management software features you should look for are the following : Vehicle tracking: Monitor vehicle locations in real time using GPS, geofencing, live ETAs, and mileage data. Driver behavior and safety monitoring: Identify risky driving patterns, improve accountability, and support regulatory safety practices. Fleet maintenance and scheduling: Plan preventive maintenance, receive service alerts, reduce downtime, and track vehicle health.
