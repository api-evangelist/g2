 <h2>Key Findings</h2> 
 <ol> 
  <li>According to G2's 2025 State of AI in AI Voice Assistants Survey, and based on 1,419 verified reviews, <strong>76% of AI voice assistant users report significant or transformational operational ROI</strong>. Yet, measuring that ROI simultaneously ranks as the #3 challenge. Organizations believe in the value being delivered, but cannot measure and prove it internally. There’s no doubt the benefits are real, but the proof remains a challenge.<br /><br /></li> 
  <li>Voice AI adoption is steadily growing across all segments. With <strong>88% of platforms reporting rapidly increased adoption over the past 12 months</strong> and 65% describing the current stage as "rapid adoption across industries," it’s clear that the question isn’t whether to invest in voice AI, but when.</li> 
 </ol>
