This G2 article identifies the leading electronic signature tools for enterprise organizations in 2026, with Adobe Acrobat, DocuSign, and PandaDoc emerging as the top-rated options. The author evaluates platforms on bulk sending, audit trails, compliance certifications (SOC 2, eIDAS), and integration with enterprise CRM and contract management systems. Large organizations managing high volumes of legally binding documents will find a structured comparison to guide their tool selection.
