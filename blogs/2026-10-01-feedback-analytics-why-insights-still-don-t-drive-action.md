Key findings on feedback analytics in 2026 Feedback collection is outpacing feedback action. According to G2's analysis of 10,500+ verified Feedback Analytics reviews and category demand data, buyer interest climbed 7.8% month over month, yet the vendors surveyed rate cross-functional confidence in unprompted feedback use at just 2 to 4 out of 5. Time-to-insight is solved; time-to-decision is not.
