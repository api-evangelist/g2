Key Findings Finding 1: G2's analysis of 2,770+ verified AI writing assistant reviews reveals that only 12% of buyers are high-volume or professional writers , meaning the other 88% are casual users, a split that differs from how most AI writing vendors position their products. Finding 2: Professional and high-volume writers are the buyers AI writing assistant vendors most aggressively court, and G2's analysis of 2,771 verified reviews shows that these high-volume writers are more likely than casual users to report that reviewing AI suggestions cancels out the time savings the tool promises .
