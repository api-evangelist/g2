TL;DR: Everything You Need to Know About Brand Intelligence in 2026 The primary challenge in brand intelligence is no longer analytics sophistication but channel coverage. As consumer and B2B conversations fragment across TikTok, Reddit, AI search engines, and private communities, comprehensive monitoring has become increasingly difficult to achieve with a single platform. The next evolution of brand intelligence is understanding brand visibility in AI-generated answers and dark social networks.
